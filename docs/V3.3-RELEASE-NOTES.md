# V3.3.0 Release Notes

## Engineering Knowledge & Decision Intelligence

V3.3.0 adds a structured Engineering Knowledge layer to the engineering workflow while keeping engineering authority, governance, security, and Learning Knowledge separate.

### Included

- Engineering records for PRD, REQ, NFR, DR, ADR, TDR, RSK, SEC, and RCA
- Canonical knowledge storage and graph relationships
- Deterministic retrieval and context assembly
- Knowledge-aware planning and workflow integration
- Reviewer, evidence, and traceability integration
- Knowledge-to-learning integration with explicit authority boundaries
- Governance and policy enforcement through the existing policy architecture
- End-to-end validation and measurement-integrity checks

## Phase 16 post-release validation

Phase 16 is experimental infrastructure on a dedicated branch. It does not modify the `v3.3.0` tag, merge to `master`, or create a new product release.

The accepted design used 20 independent benchmark task clusters, three treatment modes, and 10 repeated observations per task/mode, for 600 executions. Statistical inference preserved task clusters as the experimental unit.

The first accepted run was invalidated and preserved after two harness defects were found. Corrected run `V3.3-P16-FV-20260918-R2` passed the reproducibility and contamination checks. Its instrumented synthetic score produced a `NEUTRAL` Engineering Knowledge effect and a `NEUTRAL` incremental Learning effect under the frozen benchmark/configuration.

Latency, cost, safety, and governance outcomes were not instrumented in this experiment. They are reported as unavailable, not as zero. Phase 13 and V3.2 observations were not pooled with Phase 16.

## Compatibility and boundaries

- Engineering Knowledge does not replace the existing policy engine.
- Learned experience remains advisory and cannot bypass governance or security authority.
- Free-model enforcement, verification, and independent review remain mandatory.
- V3.3.0 is immutable historical evidence.
- Phase 16 results are scoped to the benchmark, model policy, dataset, and configuration used in that experiment.
